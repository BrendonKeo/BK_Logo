Logo

A collection of different logos for Brendon Keo.

Black and White

This black and white logo, is best used on light backgrounds. It's high contrast is great for print.

Color

This color logo is the primary logo for branding and is meant for digital content like webpages or general branding.

Color Plus

An alternative version to the primary logo with the "spine" and "k" also colored in the navy blue.

Color Inverse

In cases where a dark background is used, this inverse version of the "Color" logo should be used. Do not use on any blue backgrounds.

Favicon

This favicon is the "BK" lettering without the "book" decoration and is best used at small sizes, such as a website favicon.

Favicon Inverse

An inverse of the standard favicon, best used when on dark background.
